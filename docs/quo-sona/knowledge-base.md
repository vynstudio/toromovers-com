## Company

Toro Movers is a family-owned local moving company serving Orlando and Central Florida.

Website: https://toromovers.com

English and Spanish support is available where possible.

## Services

Toro Movers handles:

- Full-service moves: Toro Movers truck and moving crew.
- Labor-only loading and unloading for customer-provided rental trucks, trailers, PODs, portable storage containers, storage units, and similar equipment.
- Apartment, condo, home, same-building, single-item, special-item, POD/container, storage-unit, and rental-truck move requests.

A full-service move includes a Toro Movers truck and crew. Labor-only service means the customer provides the rental truck, trailer, POD, container, storage unit, or other location, and Toro Movers provides moving labor.

## Service area

Toro Movers serves Orlando and nearby Central Florida communities, including Winter Park, Kissimmee, Oviedo, Clermont, Winter Garden, Sanford, Lake Mary, Altamonte Springs, and St. Cloud.

Locations outside the normal service area require team confirmation.
